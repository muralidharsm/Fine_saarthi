# Honest limitations, safety boundaries and future scope

This is a hackathon prototype, not production financial software. It intentionally keeps judge-visible demonstrations real and avoids false claims.

**Already interactive:** invoice entry and editing, CSV import/export, text extraction from searchable PDFs, transparent document checks, overdue alerts, financial aggregation, adjustable weekly cash-flow projections, credit-document checklist, local guidance assistant, optional consent-based LLM integration.

**Not implemented:** OCR of photos/scanned PDFs, automatic GST filing or statutory due-date computation, direct bank/GST portal connections, bank transaction sync, persistence across sessions, secure user accounts, real credit evaluation, regulated investment/lending advice, ML-trained forecasting.

**Before use with real business data:** implement authentication, encrypted persistent storage, access control, logging, retention and consent, document-level data validation, regulator-approved tax logic, secure secret management and expert review.

All sample data and numeric KPIs in the demo are synthetic. There are no invented pilots, customers, product adoption metrics or revenue claims.
