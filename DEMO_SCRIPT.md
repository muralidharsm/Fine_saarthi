**Presenter:** MURALIDHAR SM · **Team:** CODE TITAN · **Solo participant**

**00:00–00:25 | Hook:** “A micro-enterprise owner should focus on customers, not search through bills and spreadsheets. Our selected problem statement is 3.1. FinSaarthi AI helps bring scattered finance tasks into one simple workflow.”

**00:25–00:55 | Command Center:** Open Command Center. Show sales, receivables, supplier payables, documents to review, and charts. Say: “These are synthetic demo figures, so the workflow is repeatable and auditable.”

**00:55–01:25 | Invoice Studio:** Switch to Invoice Studio → Add an invoice. Enter a demo invoice, select due date, and save. Go to ledger and filter for Unpaid. Show CSV export or selectable-text PDF extraction. Explain that human review prevents unsafe automatic entries.

**01:25–01:55 | Compliance Hub:** Open the review queue. Point out missing GSTIN, invoice tax amount checks, and overdue reminders. Clarify that it is a preparation assistant, not tax filing software.

**01:55–02:25 | Cash-Flow Lab:** Move the weekly-cost slider until a risk warning appears. Explain scenario-based cash-flow visibility and how owners can test better and worse assumptions.

**02:25–02:45 | Credit Passport and assistant:** Click the document checklist, download the passport, then ask “What invoices are overdue?” Demonstrate local context-aware responses without an API key.

**02:45–03:00 | Close:** “FinSaarthi AI turns fragmented records into clear next steps. Next: validate with 25 local MSMEs, add secure persistence, verified OCR and CA-reviewed compliance workflows.”

## Anticipated questions

- **Is this AI today?** The demo implements real workflow logic, context-based responses and text-based PDF parsing. Optional external LLM support is available with explicit consent; production OCR and advanced forecasting are roadmap items.
- **What is different?** A daily owner-oriented decision cockpit combining ledger, document review, financial scenarios and business guidance rather than a single tax form.
- **What is your moat?** Micro-enterprise focused UX, multilingual workflows, distribution through trusted local accountants; these are hypotheses for field validation.
- **How do you measure impact?** Time saved per invoice, extraction corrections, review coverage, weekly active use and 30-day retention in a controlled pilot.
